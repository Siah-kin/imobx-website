The same method applies to construction and suppliers. A builder linked to a founder needs to disclose that relationship. Other people compare the price, approve the contract and inspect the completed work. Disclosure alone does not resolve the conflict of interest.

A vote, including a token vote, cannot on its own change a family’s housing agreement. Residents’ say does not depend on investing, and investors receive only the rights written in their agreement.

## Sources support specific claims

Reviewed on 17 October 2026. Official sources explain rules and data. They do not approve IMOBX. A provider’s documentation describes its product, without establishing an IMOBX integration. Founder-supplied figures retain that attribution.

[Rafael: budget and loft account](https://siah-kin.github.io/imobx-website/whitepaper.html#3-the-cost-proposal-already-supplied)

Founder information dated 30 November 2025 and 20 January 2026. Public summary without private correspondence. It is not a final-cost report or independent assessment.

[IBGE: 2022 Census, favelas and urban communities](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/41797-censo-2022-brasil-tinha-16-4-milhoes-de-pessoas-morando-em-favelas-e-comunidades-urbanas)

16.4 million residents, 8.1% of the population, in the 2022 Census. This does not itself measure the housing deficit or describe each household.

[CAIXA: SINAPI](https://www.caixa.gov.br/poder-publico/modernizacao-gestao/sinapi/paginas/default.aspx)

Brazilian reference for construction costs and inputs. A specific budget needs a location, date and scope.

[Elinor Ostrom: Nobel lecture](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/economic-sciences/2009/ostrom/lecture/)

Research basis for shared-resource governance principles. Their application to IMOBX is the project’s proposal.

[ICA: cooperative identity](https://ica.coop/en/cooperatives/cooperative-identity)

Democratic member control and autonomy. Does not establish that IMOBX is already a registered cooperative.

[CVM: Guidance Opinion 40](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/legislacao/pareceres-orientacao/pare040.html)

CVM, Brazil’s securities-market regulator, examines token characteristics and rights. Utility or governance labels do not authorise an offer.

[CVM: Resolution 249 of 13 October 2026](https://conteudo.cvm.gov.br/legislacao/resolucoes/anexos/200/resol249.htm)

The experimental programme under article 1, paragraph 3 excludes real investors, actually issued securities and real-money movements in its tests. It does not authorise public offerings or other regulated activities.

[Law 13.019: definition for public-sector partnerships](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2011-2014/2014/lei/l13019compilado.htm)

Article 2 defines nonprofit organisations for that law. The fit between social purpose and any investment offered needs specific legal review.

[Arc: official documentation](https://docs.arc.io/)

Network features and tools for digital payments. Does not establish IMOBX use, partnership or regulatory approval.

[Arctide · Arc-based exchange](https://arctide.app/)

An example of a trading platform on the Arc network. It does not establish IMOBX use.

[Fundação João Pinheiro: 2024 housing deficit](https://fjp.mg.gov.br/deficit-habitacional-recua-34-no-brasil-e-soma-5-773-983-domicilios/)

An estimated 5,773,983 homes in the 2024 deficit. This measures housing needs under its own criteria. It differs from the 2022 Census count of people living in favelas.

[FTC: disclosing brand relationships](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers)

US guidance on disclosing paid relationships. It does not authorise investment offers or replace rules in other countries reached.

[X: paid-partnerships policy](https://help.x.com/en/rules-and-policies/paid-partnerships-policy)

Requires paid-partnership disclosure and prohibits content categories. Country rules include crypto and financial products in the EU, UK and Australia. A disclosure does not make prohibited content permissible.

[Argus · Arc-based exchange](https://argus.world/)

An example of a trading platform on the Arc network. It does not establish IMOBX use.

## How to cite this reference

Citation format: IMOBX, Project reference, v0.8, reviewed 24 October 2026, followed by the section title and link. The version file identifies documents in this edition. The date records content review. It does not confirm publication, signature or construction start.

Each correction needs a date and a named source. A new decision also needs the name of the person who approved it and a link to its basis.

In this edition, founder figures remain attributed to him. The site does not automatically monitor balances or legal changes.
