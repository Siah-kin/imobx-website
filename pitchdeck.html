01 / THE PROJECT

A home. An agreement. A record of the money.

IMOBX is preparing a Rio housing pilot with a written rent-to-own pathway and project reporting.

  • Ground work: construction, property access and resident operations.
  • Digital work: clear information, questions and approved reports.
  • Current stage: design and evidence review.

02 / THE PEOPLE

Start with households and the home.

The intended pilot serves households displaced or excluded from ordinary housing finance.

  • Define eligibility and affordability with local participants.
  • Explain payments, maintenance and the ownership path in ordinary Portuguese.
  • Provide a support and dispute route residents can actually use.

04 / THE OPERATING MODEL

One accountable organization coordinates the home.

Property control, resident agreements and builder responsibilities must connect in writing.

  • Document ownership or operating authority for the selected property.
  • Contract the builder and disclose related-party terms.
  • Specify what the resident pays, earns toward ownership and receives at completion.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT04 / 12
Presenter notes

A proposed donation or memorandum is not a completed property transfer. A token is not the title record. The legal form and transfer arrangement need Brazilian professional review.

05 / MONEY

Keep each source and use visible.

Rent, grants, service receipts and trading fees enter the books separately.

  • Pay taxes, collection and agreed property/organization costs.
  • Fund repairs, resident commitments, vacancies and reserves.
  • Commit growth capital under an agreed rule; assess any lawful surplus afterward.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT05 / 12
Presenter notes

Historical 98% rental distributions are not adopted here. Resident purchase credits are obligations or entitlements, not extra cash. A resale payment belongs to the seller except for separately collected fees. Locked trading liquidity is not available construction cash.

06 / CONTINUITY

Self-sufficiency has two tests.

Cover the homes already operating. Then finance the next cohort.

  • Test full operating costs through low or zero trading activity.
  • Account for rent ending when a home transfers to its resident.
  • Identify retained assets or recurring services; model expansion capital separately.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT06 / 12
Presenter notes

One thousand homes is a hypothesis, not a break-even result. Grants may help eligible costs but one-off awards are not recurring income. Three- or six-year reviews do not guarantee a distribution.

07 / RESIDENT VOICE

Write down who decides what.

Residents help shape suitable local decisions; their contracts define their rights.

  • Consult on maintenance priorities, shared facilities and support.
  • Define board duties, budget authority and conflict rules.
  • Protect occupancy and the ownership path from unrelated token votes.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT07 / 12
Presenter notes

Ostrom informs the design direction: boundaries, affected users' participation, monitoring and accessible dispute resolution. There is no tenant-token allocation or adopted voting-weight plan. Compare household/member participation options with advisers and residents.

09 / TOKEN DECISION

Define the right before selecting the token.

A participation credential and a financial interest are different products.

  • Specify issuer, rights, governance scope and transfer process.
  • Review eligibility, offer language and providers with Brazilian advisers.
  • Proceed with ordinary contracts if a token adds no useful benefit.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT09 / 12
Presenter notes

CVM Guidance Opinion 40 concerns cryptoasset characteristics and rights. Do not claim SEC, MiCA or Brazilian approval. No supply, APY, sale date, tenant allocation or payout plan is agreed. Website v2 requires accepted rights and a permitted provider route.

11 / PILOT EVIDENCE

Count homes and fulfilled commitments.

The first scorecard measures delivery, cash discipline and resident experience.

  • Budget and schedule variance; property rights and resident agreement.
  • Monthly source/use/reserve reconciliation; maintenance and unresolved issues.
  • Resident participation and progress under the ownership agreement.
