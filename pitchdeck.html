01 / THE PROJECT

A home. An agreement. A record of the money.

IMOBX is preparing a Rio housing pilot with a written rent-to-own pathway and project reporting.

  • Ground work: construction, property access and resident operations.
  • Digital work: clear information, questions and approved reports.
  • Current stage: design and evidence review.

02 / THE PEOPLE

Start with households and the home.

The intended pilot serves households displaced or excluded from ordinary housing finance.

  • Define eligibility and affordability with local participants.
  • Explain payments, maintenance and the ownership path in ordinary Portuguese.
  • Provide a support and dispute route residents can actually use.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT02 / 12
Presenter notes

Brazil’s 2022 Census recorded around 16.4 million residents in favelas and urban communities, 8.1% of the population. IBGE describes community formation and identities, while UN-Habitat provides the wider inadequate-housing context. These are national/global references, not evidence of the selected pilot site. Do not describe all informal-settlement residents as displaced. Listen to households about location, work, services and their preferences. The founders’ target group requires local definition and consultation. No pilot residents are claimed as enrolled.

04 / THE OPERATING MODEL

One accountable organization coordinates the home.

Property control, resident agreements and builder responsibilities must connect in writing.

  • Document ownership or operating authority for the selected property.
  • Contract the builder and disclose related-party terms.
  • Specify what the resident pays, earns toward ownership and receives at completion.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT04 / 12
Presenter notes

A proposed donation or memorandum is not a completed property transfer. A token is not the title record. The legal form and transfer arrangement need Brazilian professional review.

05 / MONEY

Keep each source and use visible.

Rent, grants, service receipts and trading fees enter the books separately.

  • Pay taxes, collection and agreed property/organization costs.
  • Fund repairs, resident commitments, vacancies and reserves.
  • Commit growth capital under an agreed rule; assess any lawful surplus afterward.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT05 / 12
Presenter notes

Historical 98% rental distributions are not adopted here. Resident purchase credits are obligations or entitlements, not extra cash. A resale payment belongs to the seller except for separately collected fees. Locked trading liquidity is not available construction cash.

06 / CONTINUITY

Self-sufficiency has two tests.

Cover the homes already operating. Then finance the next cohort.

  • Test full operating costs through low or zero trading activity.
  • Account for rent ending when a home transfers to its resident.
  • Identify retained assets or recurring services; model expansion capital separately.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT06 / 12
Presenter notes

One thousand homes is a hypothesis, not a break-even result. Grants may help eligible costs but one-off awards are not recurring income. Three- or six-year reviews do not guarantee a distribution.

07 / RESIDENT VOICE

A voice comes from a role, not just a balance.

Consult on resident authority, delegated work and separately defined financial rights.

  • Resident voice on shared use and maintenance within agreed limits.
  • Time-limited delivery roles, conflict checks and replaceable representatives.
  • Occupancy and purchase rights stay protected by agreements and law.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT07 / 12
Presenter notes

One token, one vote on everything risks purchased control. The consultation example uses equal voice per verified member in resident matters, relevant experience for elected/appointed work roles, and financial-holder rights defined by their instrument. It is not an adopted charter. Ostrom informs boundaries, local fit, collective choice, accountable monitoring, proportionate responses, conflicts, recognized authority and nested levels. See roadmap.html#governance. Online activity or a score does not decide housing rights.

08 / DIGITAL TOOLS

Use digital records where they reduce work.

The Flock is the proposed Telegram desk. Arc is the settlement network; Arctide is a candidate application.

  • Approved rules, requests and responses make responsibilities visible.
  • Compare Arctide project fees with Argus and Uniswap alternatives.
  • SIAH/HiveDAO coordination is proposed backend work, not a deployed IMOBX claim.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT08 / 12
Presenter notes

The Flock stays neutral. Arc documents USDC gas; Arctide documents token/USDC pairs and configured fee recipients. Argus also documents USDC distributions and accumulator dividends; Uniswap v4 supports custom hooks. No exclusive-reward or universal-cost advantage is established. Trial receipt reconciliation, permissions, support costs and provider replacement. No automated agent acquires authority over homes by being called a mentor.

09 / TOKEN DECISION

Separate participation from financial entitlement.

A mission-led nonprofit operator and an investor dividend need distinct legal consideration.

  • Choose the legal form and protect restricted donations and resident commitments.
  • Define any financial claim, issuer, cash source and permitted offer/transfer route.
  • Reinvestment may grow operations; it is not automatic token yield or appreciation.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT09 / 12
Presenter notes

Under Law 13.019's nonprofit definition for public-sector partnerships, results support the social purpose rather than distributions. Do not promise the nonprofit's profits as investor dividends. Consider participation without profit rights or a separately permitted financial arrangement with Brazilian advisers; no structure is adopted. A DAO/governance label does not settle securities treatment. No supply, APY, sale date or tenant allocation is agreed.

10 / THE ROADMAP

Evidence gates, with parallel work streams.

The distinct roadmap links housing delivery, governance and useful digital services.

  • Days 1–30: roles, entity/property evidence, scoped budget and consultation.
  • Days 31–60: cash rules and decision exercises; day 90: readiness decision.
  • Funded pilot → operating evidence → token decision and repeatable delivery.
IMOBX / PARTNER DISCUSSION DRAFT10 / 12
Presenter notes

Proposed preparation targets start only from an agreed date; construction needs funding, property and permits. Observe at least three operating months before the first replication review. Website v1 is published; operational v2 reports real delivery/cash and does not require a token. Optional v3 token access follows rights, legal/provider and technical verification. See roadmap.html and faq.html. A second local team must repeat the process; one thousand homes does not prove self-sufficiency.

11 / PILOT EVIDENCE

Count homes and fulfilled commitments.

The first scorecard measures delivery, cash discipline and resident experience.

  • Budget and schedule variance; property rights and resident agreement.
  • Monthly source/use/reserve reconciliation; maintenance and unresolved issues.
  • Resident participation and progress under the ownership agreement.
