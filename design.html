IMOBX visual identity and images

Brand reference · 16 October 2026

IMOBX’s visual identity uses its supplied house-shaped logo, white space and a restrained teal palette. The website presents the housing proposal alongside local photographs and architectural drawings.

Logo

The supplied logo combines a house symbol with the IMOBX name. Its colours, proportions and lettering form one piece of artwork. Clear space around it keeps it legible.

  • Logo for light backgrounds, PNG
  • Logo for dark backgrounds, JPG
  • Original light artwork, WebP

The website and documents use the light version on white. No editable vector artwork is included.

Photographs and drawings

The city photographs show favelas along the hillsides. Circles highlight the favelas in each view.

The village illustration and loft drawings show proposed designs. Construction photographs show the site and building work.

Project descriptions

The media kit provides short descriptions in Portuguese and English. The project paper explains the housing proposal, costs and current stage. These descriptions identify the project as being prepared, with its legal form and token rights still being decided.
